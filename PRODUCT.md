# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Zyene administrators and employees using a private internal outreach tool during
the workday. Administrators manage access. Employees configure their own purpose,
audience and connections, then manage contacts, sequences and campaign runs.

## Product Purpose

LeadZen by Zyene supports outreach for Zyene products, including Zyene Reviews,
and Zyene services. Success means employees can identify their next task, review
email activity and manage outreach without exposing another employee's data.
The intended production address is leadzen.zyene.com; support is support@zyene.com.

## Business Time

The entire platform uses **America/New_York (Eastern Time)**, with automatic
daylight-saving changes. Employees can edit weekly sending days and start/end
hours, but cannot select another timezone. Discovery, outreach, daily totals and
displayed dates use this same clock regardless of employee country or computer
timezone. Stored timestamps remain UTC instants. Changing approved timing requires
a fresh review before automatic outreach resumes.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, precise. LeadZen uses Zyene's ink-and-paper materials,
typography and controls so the product feels like a working screen within Zyene.
Preserve the existing Workspace and Chat layouts, with easy scanning and
dependable controls. The exact visual specification is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy decoration, landing-page-sized headings, saturated panels, generic
hero metrics, invented analytics, confusing controls and visually inconsistent
pages. This is an operational workspace, not a marketing presentation.

## Design Principles

- Make the next task easy to find; preserve familiar navigation and controls.
- Keep administration simple and focused on employee accounts.
- Show real state and real data; never decorate the dashboard with invented charts.
- Make account, campaign and delivery states readable before an action is taken.
- Treat credentials and employee workspaces as private boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The user confirmed keyboard access and reduced-motion support. Use explicit
focus states, readable text contrast and labels alongside status colors. Aim for
WCAG AA contrast, maintain 44px touch targets, and support narrow viewports
without hiding necessary account-management controls.
