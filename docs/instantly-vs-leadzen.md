# Instantly vs LeadZen

**Product name:** This checkout is the LeadZen by Zyene platform (the OpenOutreach project).  
**Checked:** October 5, 2026

Connect a data provider for discovery and optional verified work-email enrichment, or import contacts. Provider and AI usage may cost extra. [SuperSearch](https://help.instantly.ai/en/articles/11364248-supersearch) | Finds profiles through the configured finder/provider. Email lookup is a separate, visible, credit-approved action; discovery without email lookup does not buy addresses. [`leadzen/discovery.py`](../leadzen/discovery.py) |

Its execution flag defaults off in source; this comparison does not claim a live service is enabled. AI provider charges may apply. [`leadzen/discovery_progress.py`](../leadzen/discovery_progress.py) · [`leadzen/chat/tools.py`](../leadzen/chat/tools.py) |

Budget separately for hosting, mailbox service, AI calls and prospect/email-data providers. |
| **Best fit** | Teams that want a managed outbound engine with a large built-in lead database, warmup, many sending inboxes and high-volume campaign controls. | Teams that want AI to find and qualify target-fit prospects, then manage personalized outreach in a company-controlled, self-hostable workspace—with provider costs and mailbox operations handled separately. |

## Positioning in one sentence

**Instantly is stronger for running outbound at scale; LeadZen is stronger for automatically finding and qualifying the right prospects, with controlled outreach attached.** LeadZen includes outreach and automation, but its current code does not provide Instantly's bundled database, inbox warmup or per-employee inbox rotation.
