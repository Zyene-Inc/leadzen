# Code of Conduct

We want the LeadZen project to be a respectful, practical place to collaborate. Treat contributors, users, and operators with respect. Give feedback about the work, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on solving the problem.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@zyene.com](mailto:support@zyene.com). Zyene may remove content or limit participation when conduct conflicts with this standard.
